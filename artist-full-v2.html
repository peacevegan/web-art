About

"Color is not something we see — it is something we feel. Each stroke is a conversation between chaos and control."

Born in 1976, he spent most of his time working in his studio in Hanoi. Working primarily in oil and mixed media, the practice explores the boundary between figuration and pure abstraction — where serpentine forms, layered color fields, and fragmented portraits emerge from dense, tactile surfaces.

Recent work draws on zodiac symbolism, natural landscapes, and the human figure as entry points into emotionally charged compositions that resist singular interpretation.

Studio

The studio in Hanoi houses large-scale canvases, wood panels, and an extensive pigment collection. Works are developed in series, often over several months, allowing layers to build organically.
